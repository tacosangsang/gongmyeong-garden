STYLE: a night garden drawn only with self-luminous points and thin light lines, not a photographic scene and not neon. Every light glows by itself; nothing is lit from outside. Points line up into rows, dotted lines and contours. Most of the frame stays dark. The people are built of fine light points of their own color, denser along the outline, darker inside, faces without detail. Quiet, ceremonial, warm then cooling; something always moves.

SUMMARY: In the flowering garden of @image1, light rises from the ground and builds five people, each the color of one tree or flower. They turn to their own tree or flower and commune with it, then walk out of the frame, red, gold and pink to the left, blue and violet to the right. Left alone, the garden loses its color into the ground, its forms dissolve, and the dotted city with gray walking people of @image2 returns. A 15-second sequence in one continuous shot.

SPACE AND SCREEN DIRECTION: one place, camera locked off on the framing of @image1 until 12s. Screen left and screen right are fixed. The five places, from screen left to right, as in @image3: cornflower blue by the cornflower shrub, camellia red against the red trunk, azalea pink beside the azalea shrub, violet by the violet trunk, gold beside the gold trunk.

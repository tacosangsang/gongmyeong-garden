@image2: the garden seen from the front at 3s: the red camellia tree in front and the gold marigold tree on the right are lit; the violet tree in the center back, the hydrangea tree at the upper right, the pink azalea bush in the middle and the cornflower bush on the left are still bare silver and teal with no flowers. @image4 and @image5: the half-open stage of an azalea and of a hydrangea. Use them only for how far those flowers have opened in the middle of the second shot. Do not copy their black backgrounds or centered framing.
Priority: gold tree first, then start the garden on @image2, open the flowers in the order written, end on @image3.

STYLE: a night void drawn only with glowing points and thin lines; not a photographic scene and not neon. Every light source glows by itself; nothing is lit from outside. Points are small round grains of light that always line up into rows, dotted lines and contours; they never look like randomly sprinkled powder, sand or dust. Lines are thin and taper toward their ends. At least 70 percent of every frame is dark, there is only one brightest thing in the frame, and white areas stay tiny. Bloom only softens thin lines; it never builds shapes. Petals are dotted lattices of light points of their flower's color, never glossy solid surfaces. The sky is deep navy turning black toward the top. Quiet and ceremonial; something in the frame always moves, even when the camera is still.

SUMMARY: Two shots. Shot 1 (0-3s): at the base of a second tree a thin warm gold line of light rises out of the dotted ground and climbs its silver trunk; the crown answers in warm gold, blooming with marigold pompoms like @image1. Shot 2 (3-13s), one continuous frontal shot: the garden answers the red and gold trees. One by one the bare plants call their own color up from the ground, open through the half-open stage of @image4 and @image5, and reach the full bloom of @image3; each blooming plant sends two or three thin rings of light across the dotted ground, overlapping into interference patterns.

TIMELINE:
0-1.5s, Shot 1, Camera: 1 m in front of the base of the second tree, 30 cm high, wide lens, starting to tilt up. A thin warm gold line of light rises out of the ground dots at the tree base and climbs the smooth silver-gray trunk like a vein; the camera follows its tip upward. Only the line is gold; the crown is still dark. Sound: <low hum>.
1.5-3s, Camera: continues tilting up to 60 degrees and slows. The crown lights up in warm gold: marigold pompoms like @image1 open across the branches from the trunk outward, never all at once, and keep twinkling. Sound: <soft rising chime>. Hard cut.
3-4s, Shot 2, Camera: locked, exactly @image2. Two thin gold rings spread from the base of the gold tree across the dotted ground.
4-7s, Camera: same. Pale lavender-blue points rise from the ground up the hydrangea tree at the upper right and gather into small knots at its branch tips; the knots open from the outside in into round domes of tiny four-petal florets, as in @image5, then fully. Thin lavender rings leave its base. At the same time violet points climb the violet tree in the center back and gather into buds. Sound: <rising soft shimmer>.
7-10s, Camera: same. The violet tree opens into five-petal violet flowers. Magenta-pink points climb the azalea bush in the middle; buds unfurl into narrow funnels, as in @image4, then into open azaleas with thin stamens. Blue points climb the cornflower bush on the left; florets peek out into rings of fringed blue petals. Each blooming plant sends thin rings of its own color across the ground; where rings cross, the dotted rows show soft interference patterns. Subtext: everyone who passed here answers. Sound: <soft chimes, one per plant>.
10-13s, Camera: same, a barely visible slow push. Every plant is in full bloom as in @image3. The rings fade into the dotted rows; thin ribbons of light mist drift between the crowns; petals keep twinkling and the willows sway slightly.

CONSISTENCY: one cut only, at 3s. In shot 1 only gold, silver and navy; no red light on the second tree. In shot 2 the red and gold trees stay as they are from 3s; trees, branches and bushes keep the shapes and places of @image2; only flowers and color grow. Each plant keeps its own single color: hydrangea pale lavender-blue, violet deep violet, azalea magenta-pink, cornflower blue. Bloom order goes from right to left and back to front. Flowers grow only forward and only on their own plant; color never spreads to trunks, leaves or ground except the thin rings. No people anywhere.

AVOID: red line or red swell in shot 1, gold flowers before 1.5s, all plants blooming at once, flowers on the bare plants at 3s, color spilling across the screen, rainbow wash, flowers closing, wilting, falling petals, plants moving or changing shape, new trees, people, text, sharp neon ring lines, filled discs, camera pan or zoom in shot 2, heavy bloom, lens flare, sand, dust, grain, objects appearing out of thin air.

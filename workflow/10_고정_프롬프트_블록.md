# 10. 고정 프롬프트 블록

STYLE·AVOID는 원문 그대로 붙이고 고치지 않는다. 바꾸면 여기 원문과 바꾼 이유를 갱신한다. 정본 스타일 규칙은 Notion 스토리보드 페이지.

## STYLE · PEOPLE · CITY (영상, 11-21 노션 기준 재정립)

정본은 노션 "그래픽 지침 · 스타일 · 톤앤매너 · 무드". 옛 "dust-like, grainy" 문구는 모래 질감을 불러 폐기.

```
STYLE: a night void drawn only with glowing points and thin lines; not a photographic scene and not neon. Every light source glows by itself; nothing is lit from outside. Points are small round grains of light that always line up into rows, dotted lines and contours; they never look like randomly sprinkled powder, sand or dust. Lines are thin and taper toward their ends. At least 70 percent of every frame is dark, there is only one brightest thing in a frame, and white areas stay tiny. Bloom only softens thin lines; it never builds shapes. Colors: black void, charcoal and gray people with faint silver-blue edges, a blue-gray dotted city. The only color is camellia red #f5333d, kept saturated: to make red brighter add a white center, never turn it pink. The seed adds a silver-white lattice and a white core. Quiet, cold and ceremonial; one place to look at a time; something in the frame always moves, even when the camera is still.

PEOPLE: side silhouettes with real, natural walking, each person with their own stride and speed. The outline is made of fine light grains that grow sparser toward the inside; the body interior is nearly black; only the outline glows faintly silver-blue. Each depth lane has its own focus and brightness, and the lanes overlap.

CITY: only dotted lines and dot grids: building outlines, window dot grids, floor lines, doorways, streetlight poles, wires. 10 to 15 percent brightness and out of focus, just enough to tell it is a city. In the close-up it becomes soft bokeh points.
```

## 사람 (11-20 규칙 변경 반영)

- 입자 외곽선, 안쪽으로 갈수록 성김, 몸 안은 거의 검정, 가장자리만 은청색으로 약하게.
- 군중은 모두 다른 남녀, 키·머리·옷이 다르고 실루엣으로만 읽힌다.
- 빨간 인물: 포니테일, 짧은 원피스, 동백 빨강 `#f5333d` 입자.

```
STAGING: the crowd is scattered irregularly at uneven distances and different angles, overlapping only partly; no rows, no queue, no evenly spaced figures.
```

## 씨앗

```
the seed: a vertical cocoon pointed at top and bottom, three spiral ribbons, light filling from below, a small white core at the bottom tip; rings of red particles circle it.
```

## AVOID (공통)

```
AVOID: solid filled bodies, visible facial detail, crisp outlines, rim light, chunky dots, pink or orange tint, red on the gray people, neon, heavy bloom, lens flare, readable text. No objects appearing out of thin air.
```
클립 전용 금지는 다음 줄에 "No ~." 또는 항목 추가로.
